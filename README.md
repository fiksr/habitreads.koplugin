**HabitReads** turns your reading routine into a visual, rewarding habit with an authentic **GitHub-style 365-day contribution heatmap**, daily streak tracking, streak freeze shields, and milestone achievements right on your e-reader.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🟩 **365-Day Contribution Heatmap**: A full 52-week x 7-day annual activity grid rendered in 4 clean e-ink shades (empty, light, medium, dark, solid black for 60+ pages).
- 🔥 **Daily Reading Streaks**: Accurately tracks your current consecutive reading streak and all-time personal best.
- 🛡️ **Streak Freeze Shields**: Protects your hard-earned streak if you miss a day due to travel, illness, or rest.
- 🏆 **Milestone Achievements**:
  - 🌱 **First Step**: Log your first reading pages.
  - 🦉 **Night Owl**: Read between midnight and 4 AM.
  - 🌅 **Early Bird**: Read before 7 AM.
  - 🏃 **Reading Marathon**: Read 50+ pages in one day.
  - ⚔️ **Centurion**: Read 100+ pages in one day.
  - 🔥 **7-Day Streak**: One full week of daily reading.
  - 🏆 **30-Day Streak**: A full month of daily reading.
- ⚡ **Zero Setup & Offline**: No account or Wi-Fi required. Works silently in the background as you turn pages.

---

## 📸 How It Works

1. **Read as Usual**: Just open any book in KOReader and read. HabitReads automatically tracks page turns.
2. **View Your Dashboard**: Open **Tools** ➔ **HabitReads** ➔ **📊 View Annual Heatmap & Badges**.
3. **Earn Badges**: As you hit reading milestones, celebratory notifications appear on screen.

---

## 🚀 Installation

### Via Storefront / AppStore
1. Open KOReader ➔ **Tools** ➔ **App Store** (or **Storefront**).
2. Search for **HabitReads** and tap **Install**.
3. Restart KOReader.

### Manual Installation
1. Download `habitreads.koplugin.zip` from Releases.
2. Extract to:
   - **Kindle**: `/mnt/us/koreader/plugins/habitreads.koplugin/`
   - **Kobo**: `.kobo/koreader/plugins/habitreads.koplugin/`
3. Restart KOReader.
